            When I go over

            SpendGuard works with your own PayPal sandbox keys and any AI key you have. Add them here to see your real sandbox payments, send real sandbox payouts and get answers written by your AI.

              PayPal sandbox

              1. Open developer.paypal.com and log in.
              2. Go to Apps & Credentials, make sure Sandbox is selected, and create an app (type: Merchant).
              3. In the app's features, tick Transaction search and Payouts, then save.
              4. Copy the Client ID and Secret into the boxes below.

              AI model

              Use a key from any of these: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Mistral, or any OpenAI-compatible API. SpendGuard picks the provider from the key, and you can change it.

                How your keys are kept

                Keys are checked with PayPal and your AI provider, then saved on this SpendGuard server for this browser only. They are never shown again in full, never shared with other visitors, and only ever sent to PayPal and the AI provider you chose. Use PayPal sandbox keys only. Remove them any time.
